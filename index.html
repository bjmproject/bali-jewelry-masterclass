The Experience

Make something with your own hands.

When you arrive at our home in Dalung, Bali, our team will welcome you, explain how the workshop works and go over the pricing. Then we'll help you choose a design from our catalog.

Then it's your turn. With guidance from our instructors, you'll work with pure silver and make your jewelry step by step. The workshop takes around two hours and is suitable for beginners. We mainly make silver rings and bracelets. Other designs may be possible with manual approval. You can take your finished piece home the same day.

A simple experience. Something you made yourself. Something you can keep.

About BJM

Our home studio in Dalung.

Bali Jewelry Masterclass is a hands-on pure silver jewelry workshop based in our home studio in Dalung, Bali. We’re about 10 minutes from Canggu, with rice fields nearby.

We welcome guests into our home studio to make silver jewelry together. No previous experience is needed — our instructors guide you through the process.

The Process

How your piece comes together.

Our instructors guide you through each step.

01

Design

Browse our wide selection of jewelry samples and find a design you like. Choose your favorite, then make it your own in silver.

02

Measure

We prepare the silver and measure the amount needed for your chosen design.

03

Melt

We heat the silver until it melts, ready to be worked into your piece.

04

Shape

We roll and shape the silver to create the form of your chosen design.

05

Solder

We join the silver pieces using heat, carefully connecting each part of the design.

06

File

We smooth the edges and surfaces, refining the shape and removing rough areas.

07

Polish

We polish the piece to smooth the surface and bring out the natural finish of the silver.

08

Finish

We add the final details, check your piece, and prepare your finished jewelry to wear.

The Craft

Working with pure silver.

Each guest works with up to 6 grams of pure silver to make one catalog design. Our instructors will show you how to measure, shape, and finish your piece.
