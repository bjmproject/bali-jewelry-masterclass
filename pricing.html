THE WORKSHOP

PRICING

Made by you. Yours to take home.

This isn’t a workshop where you simply choose a finished piece from a display. You’ll start with silver, work with your own hands, and create a piece of jewelry with guidance from our instructors at our home studio in Bali.

Come on your own, with someone special, or with a group of friends. The workshop stays the same — only the price changes depending on your group size.

SINGLE

IDR 650,000

per person

Come on your own.

You’ll get the same hands-on workshop experience, with guidance from our instructor whenever you need it.

Choose your design, learn the process, and make a piece of silver jewelry of your own.

COUPLE

IDR 600,000

per person

Two people. Two pieces.

Come with your partner, your best friend, or someone you’d simply like to share the workshop with.

You’ll each make your own piece of silver jewelry while working side by side through the process.

SMALL GROUP

3–10 PEOPLE

IDR 575,000

per person

Bring a few friends or family.

Everyone makes their own piece while our instructors guide the group through the process. You’ll work alongside each other, share ideas, and learn the same jewelry-making techniques together.

LARGE GROUP

11–20 PEOPLE

IDR 550,000

per person

Bring the whole group.

Traveling together, celebrating something, or looking for an activity everyone can take part in? We can welcome groups of up to 20 guests while keeping the workshop hands-on and guided.

Each guest makes their own piece of jewelry with support from our instructors throughout the workshop.

WHAT’S INCLUDED

Everything you need to make your piece.

You don’t need to bring tools, materials, or previous jewelry-making experience. We prepare the workspace and guide you from choosing your design through to finishing your jewelry.

Up to 6g of silver

A silver material allowance is included for each guest.

One finished piece per guest

Choose one jewelry design from our workshop catalog, make it during the workshop, and take it home with you.

2-hour workshop

The standard workshop includes two hours to make and finish your jewelry.

Tools & equipment

Everything needed for the standard workshop is provided.

Instructor guidance

Our instructors guide you through each stage and assist with techniques that require extra care.

No experience needed

The workshop is designed for complete beginners. You’ll learn as you make.

WANT TO MAKE IT MORE PERSONAL?

Your design can go beyond the standard workshop.

Want something heavier, more detailed, or a little different from the standard design? Depending on the piece, we may be able to accommodate additional silver, gemstones, custom work, plating, or other details.

Additional silver IDR 95,000 / gram

Additional silver beyond the included 6 grams is charged per gram.

Special additions Priced by design

Gemstones, custom work, plating, and other special additions may involve an additional cost depending on the materials, design, and work required.

Additional time IDR 50,000 / 30 minutes

If your piece needs more than the standard two hours to complete, additional workshop time is available in 30-minute increments.

Any additional requirement and estimated cost will be explained before the work begins. No surprise charges at the end.

NOT SURE WHICH OPTION TO BOOK?

Just book for the number of people coming.

The workshop experience is the same for every guest. The different prices simply reflect the number of people attending together.
